The orb is your recovery.

A glass sphere with ribbons of light moving through it. It beats at a resting heart rhythm, and its colours shift with your recovery zone. Every screen below works in light and dark. Switch the theme in the top right corner.

The orb is a real-time GPU shader. In the app it becomes a Metal shader on a SwiftUI view, so it looks the same on iPhone and Apple Watch. All numbers are Lumen demo data.
