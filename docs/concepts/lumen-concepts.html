Four directions for Lumen

Each direction replaces the three plain rings with a living recovery orb: an iris that pulses at a resting heart rhythm, dilates on every beat and throws an anamorphic light streak. Same data in all four (recovery 71 %, sleep 78 %, strain 16.9). Pick one and the app gets rebuilt around it.
